Setting up Sieve

Sieve Welcome

01 · Welcome

A quieter internet, set up in a minute.

Sieve sifts out the worst of the web — scams, gambling, manipulative design, toxic comments, endless feeds — and it does all of it on this device.

One question below gets you protected. Everything else can wait.

02 · What it does

Four kinds of filter, one quiet tool.

Sites
Gambling, scams, malware, piracy and more — stopped before the page loads, with lists that keep themselves current.
Pages
Profanity masked, toxic comments hidden, dark patterns, ads and trackers taken out of the pages you still visit.
Attention
A daily limit on endless feeds, a pause screen when you reach it, and a YouTube with the distractions removed.
Control
An optional PIN, so switching protection off always takes a deliberate moment — yours or anyone else’s.

04 · Privacy

Nothing leaves this browser.

Sieve runs entirely on your device. Nothing you browse, type, or block is sent anywhere, there is no account, and your settings stay here.

You can change any of this, any time, in Settings.
